Deep Drop - Sea Battle Royale (3D HTML5 prototype)

HOW TO RUN
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (desktop).
   For best mouse-look, run a local server instead:
     python3 -m http.server 8000   then open http://localhost:8000
3. An internet connection is needed: the game loads Three.js (r128)
   from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
4. Click anywhere once to enable sound (browsers block audio until you interact).

THE DROP
You start on a plane crossing the sea. Press SPACE over your chosen spot to jump.
In freefall: WASD steer, Shift dive faster, SPACE opens the parachute early
(it opens automatically near the water). Land, then dive in for a splashdown.

MOBILE
On a phone the touch controls appear automatically (landscape): left thumb floating stick (push to the rim to sprint),
drag the right side to look, hold FIRE (drag to aim), AIM toggle, up/down to rise or dive, tap weapon slots to swap.
To preview on a desktop browser add ?touch=1 to the address.

SETTINGS
Open Settings from the lobby (or the pause screen) to pick Auto/Low/Medium/High or tune resolution, water,
clouds, caustics, lights, seagrass, fish, effects, view distance and the FPS counter.

CONTROLS
W A S D move / drive boat     Space rise    C dive    Shift sprint
Click or F shoot              Right-click aim
R reload   Q or 1/2 swap gun  E board/leave boat   H use oxygen+health kit
G grow coral wall   B air-pocket dome (both found as pickups in deep zones)
M mute / unmute               Esc pause
X toggle continuous forward swimming

PHYSICS
Swimmers have momentum and water drag, ocean currents push everything, boats have thrust, keel and wave buoyancy,
rocks, props, walls and the old ship are solid, bullets stop on them, and defeated divers sink as ragdolls.

SEA SURFACE
Layered swell, mid waves, chop and ripples with foam, sun glints, depth colour and a sky dome;
floating seaweed mats ride the waves and seagrass meadows sway in the shallows.

THE SEA
Nine themed deep zones: Bioluminescent Garden, Crystal Caverns, Thermal Vents,
Kelp Forest & Whale Fall, Sunken Ruins, Orb Abyss, Coral Cathedral, Treasure Cove,
and the Ghost Ship Graveyard with a large old ship floating on the surface.

GOAL
Loot guns, fish coins and kits in the deep-sea zones, watch your oxygen,
and be the last diver afloat before the red zone closes in.
